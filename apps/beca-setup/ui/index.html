    how does BECA work?

    Connect the electrodes to a leaf and the soil, then open Performance to play. Route MIDI to your DAW here in Setup. Aux sound comes from the device’s audio socket.

    Ready to record

    Choose a virtual MIDI destination above, then select that same name as the MIDI input in your DAW. Load an instrument and arm the track.

    On Windows, create a port named BECA in loopMIDI and enable its autostart. On Mac/Linux, choose BECA (virtual MIDI) above. Keep BECA open while playing.

    For hardware synths, choose their MIDI output directly. Microsoft GS Wavetable Synth plays the computer’s built-in sounds; it does not send MIDI to your DAW.
